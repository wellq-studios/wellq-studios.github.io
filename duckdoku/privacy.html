01About this notice

This notice covers Duckdoku, the duck logic puzzle game for Android published by WellQ Studios on Google Play (app ID com.wellq.duckdoku). WellQ Studios is an eenmanszaak based in Limburg, Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under KvK number 42201662. Our business correspondence address is Lavendelstraat 16, Weert, Netherlands. You can reach us at info@wellq.io.

WellQ Studios is responsible for the game. Google is responsible for the information it collects through its advertising service, as explained in section 04. Messages you send to info@wellq.io are covered by our website Privacy Notice.

02The short version

  • No account. You can play without signing up, logging in, or giving us your name or email.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, scores, settings, and your randomly generated player name are saved on your device. We don't receive them.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device information and identifiers to show and measure ads.
  • You choose about personalised ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • We don't run our own analytics. We have turned off Unity's analytics, crash reporting, and performance reporting services, and we don't sell your information.

03Information kept on your device

To make the game work, Duckdoku saves these small files in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Progress: the highest level you have completed, your best result per level, your no-mistake streak, and whether you have finished the how-to-play lesson.
  • Player card: a player name the game generates at random (for example, six letters and numbers), plus the duck colour and frame you pick. You don't type in a real name.
  • Daily streak: the dates you solved a puzzle, so the game can count consecutive days.
  • Hints: how many hints you have, and codes for reward videos already credited, so a reward is never counted twice.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, and display choices.
  • Rating prompt: whether the game has already asked you to rate it, so it doesn't keep asking.

This information is not sent to WellQ Studios, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, and we have no access to it.

The leaderboard in this version shows sample players created on your device, labelled as samples. Your player name and results are not uploaded. If we add online features later, we will update this notice first and explain what is shared.

04Advertising

Duckdoku is free to play and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The game shows a banner below the puzzle, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos that you can choose to watch for an extra hint or an extra egg. Full-screen ads never interrupt a puzzle, and reward videos play only when you choose them.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google's software in the app collects information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language, and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, clicked, or watched to the end;
  • diagnostic information about how ads load and perform.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you allow it, to personalise ads. Google acts as an independent controller for this information. Its use is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

With your consent, Google may work with other advertising partners that are listed in the consent screen. Information is encrypted while it is sent. WellQ Studios receives only summary reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown and the resulting earnings. These reports do not identify you.

05Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: when you first open the game, a consent message from Google's consent tool asks whether ad partners may store or access information on your device and use it for personalised ads. If you don't agree, you can still play everything. Google may then show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less information, such as your approximate location and the app you are using.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising (the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under those laws).
  • Change your mind at any time: open Settings → Privacy options in the game to review or withdraw your choices. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.
  • On your phone: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; names vary by device).
  • Reward videos are always optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

06Google Play and other links

You download and update Duckdoku through Google Play, which Google operates under its own terms and privacy policy. Google may share anonymous statistics with us through the Google Play Console, such as installs, ratings, and technical crash and performance reports. These reports help us fix problems and do not identify you to us.

If you choose to rate the game, Duckdoku opens its Google Play page, and Google Play's policies apply there. Links to this notice and to our websites open in your browser.

07Why information is processed

  • Running the game: the files on your device are needed to provide the game you asked for, and they stay on your device.
  • Personalised ads and storing or accessing information on your device for ads: we rely on your consent where the law requires it, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, ad measurement, and fraud prevention: these support our legitimate interest in offering the game for free and protecting it from abuse, balanced against your rights. Where consent is required for these, we ask for it through the consent message.
  • Answering your messages: our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

08Sharing and international processing

We don't sell your information or give it to data brokers. The services involved in Duckdoku are:

  • Google (AdMob and the consent tool): ads and consent, as described above.
  • Google (Google Play): distribution, updates, and anonymous app statistics.

Google may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google describes its safeguards for these transfers, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, in its privacy information. Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

09Retention and security

Game data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Your consent choices are also stored on your device so the game remembers them, and you can change them at any time. Google keeps advertising information according to its own retention policies, which are described in the Google retention information. Emails are kept as described in our website Privacy Notice.

Game files are stored in storage that other apps cannot read, and ad information is encrypted while it is sent. No system is completely secure, so please keep your device's software up to date.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or port your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. These rights have conditions and exceptions.

  • For information on your device, you are in control. You can clear the app's data or uninstall the game at any time. There is no account to delete.
  • For advertising information, use the in-game Privacy options, your Android ad settings, and Google's My Ad Center. You can also contact Google directly.
  • For anything else, or if you need help, email info@wellq.io. See also how privacy requests are handled.

You may complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (the Dutch Data Protection Authority) or to the supervisory authority in your own country.

11Children

Duckdoku is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and it is listed on Google Play for that age group. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the game, contact us at info@wellq.io.

12Changes and contact

We will update this notice when the game or its services change, for example if we add purchases, online leaderboards, or analytics. Where required, we will tell you about material changes in the game or on Google Play. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Questions? Write to info@wellq.io. See also the Duckdoku Terms of Service.
