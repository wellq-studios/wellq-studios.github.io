This text is tailored to the current website. The business name, business type, KvK number, correspondence address, and contact email have been confirmed by the studio. Hosting and email arrangements and retention practices still need confirmation before this text can be finalised.

01About this notice

This notice describes the current WellQ Studios landing page and its small cat spotting toy. It also addresses messages sent to info@wellq.io. WellQ Studios is an eenmanszaak based in Limburg, Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under KvK number 42201662. Our business correspondence address is Lavendelstraat 16, Weert, Netherlands.

This notice does not cover future mobile games. Before a game launches, its privacy information must describe the actual SDKs, advertising, analytics, purchases, age requirements, and platform services it uses. For help exercising your privacy rights, see Data Protection.

02Information involved

  • Website delivery: loading a website involves technical information such as your IP address, requested pages, browser information, and request time being received by hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may maintain access or security logs. Its exact settings and retention remain to be verified.
  • Messages: contacting us by email shares your email address, message, and any attachments you send. Clicking an email link opens your email application; it does not automatically send a message.
  • Website toy: the current toy keeps its round and score in page memory. Its code does not send gameplay results to a server or save them in browser storage. Reloading the page resets play.
  • Copy email: clicking the copy button asks your browser to write our email address to your clipboard. It does not read your clipboard.

03Fonts, cookies, and external services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google to request font files, which involves sending technical request information, including an IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information.

The site code does not add analytics tools, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cookies. This is not a claim that hosting or access services use no cookies. The current private preview is hosted through OpenAI Sites and may require authentication managed by that platform. Its access and security processing is separate from the toy code.

Any future addition of optional tracking requires an updated notice and, where required, a consent choice before that tracking starts. External websites and your email service have their own privacy practices.

04Why information is processed

Necessary website delivery, security, and responding to general enquiries may rely on legitimate interests in operating a safe website and communicating with visitors, subject to their rights. Where you ask about a contract or engagement, relevant correspondence may be necessary to take steps at your request before a contract. Records may also be needed to meet a specific legal obligation.

Any processing based on consent must make that choice clear and allow consent to be withdrawn. The purposes and legal grounds must be checked against the confirmed hosting and email arrangements before this draft is adopted.

05Providers and international processing

Hosting, font delivery, and email services can involve providers receiving personal information. This website does not include a function for selling personal information or sharing it with advertising networks.

Provider identities, contractual roles, processing locations, and any transfers outside the European Economic Area must be confirmed for the final notice. Where an international transfer requires safeguards, these may include an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. The applicable mechanism and how to obtain details must be documented.

06Retention and security

The toy’s state lasts only for the current page session. Email correspondence should be kept only as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, any resulting relationship, and applicable recordkeeping or legal needs. Concrete email and hosting log retention periods must be confirmed by the operator.

Access controls and appropriate technical and organisational measures should protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not necessary for your enquiry.

07Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, the GDPR provides rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port personal information, and to object to certain processing. Consent can be withdrawn where processing relies on it. These rights have conditions and exceptions.

Contact info@wellq.io to make a request. See how privacy requests are handled. You may complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or another competent supervisory authority. Learn more from the European Commission’s information on privacy rights.

08Children and future changes

The current website has no account registration or personal information form. If a child sends unnecessary personal information by email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal where appropriate. Future games need an age appropriate privacy assessment and notice before launch.

Changes to the website or its data practices should be reflected in this notice, with material changes communicated where required. The date above identifies this draft revision, not an effective final policy.
