01About this notice

This notice describes the current WellQ Studios landing page and its small cat spotting toy. It also addresses messages sent to info@wellq.io. WellQ Studios is an eenmanszaak based in Limburg, Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under KvK number 42201662. Our business correspondence address is Lavendelstraat 16, Weert, Netherlands.

This notice does not cover future mobile games. Before a game launches, its privacy information must describe the actual SDKs, advertising, analytics, purchases, age requirements, and platform services it uses. For help exercising your privacy rights, see Data Protection.

02Information involved

  • Website delivery: wellq.io is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading the website sends technical request information to the hosting infrastructure. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed into GitHub.
  • Messages: emailing info@wellq.io shares your email address, message, and any attachments. Namecheap forwards these messages to our Gmail inbox, where we read and manage them. Clicking an email link does not automatically send a message.
  • Website toy: game progress stays in page memory. The toy does not send gameplay results to a server or save them in browser storage. Reloading the page resets play.
  • Copy email: clicking the copy button asks your browser to write our email address to your clipboard. It does not read your clipboard.

03Fonts, cookies, and external services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to request font files, which involves technical request information including an IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Our website code does not add analytics tools, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cookies. Hosting and access services may process technical data separately. Private review copies hosted through OpenAI Sites may also use that platform’s authentication and security services; this is separate from the public GitHub Pages website.

If we introduce optional tracking, we will update this notice and request consent before it starts where required. External websites and your own email service have their own privacy practices.

04Why information is processed

We use contact information to answer questions, discuss collaborations, and manage correspondence. Our legal basis for general enquiries is our legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact the studio, balanced against their interests and rights. Information needed to take steps at your request before a contract is processed for that purpose.

Necessary technical processing supports our legitimate interest in delivering and securing the website. Where a specific legal obligation requires records, we retain the relevant information to meet that obligation. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

05Providers and international processing

We use the following services in operating the website and receiving messages:

  • GitHub Pages: serves website files and processes technical request information. See GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Namecheap: provides domain services and forwards messages addressed to info@wellq.io to our Gmail inbox. Forwarding involves handling message content and routing information. See the Namecheap Privacy Policy.
  • Google: provides our Gmail inbox and the website’s font delivery service. See the Google Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their linked policies describe their processing locations and transfer arrangements. GitHub describes reliance on European Commission standard contractual clauses and the Data Privacy Framework where applicable. Namecheap describes processing in the United States and references standard contractual clauses. Google describes its international transfer frameworks in its privacy information. Contact us if you would like more information about relevant safeguards.

We do not sell enquiry information or provide it to advertising networks. Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

06Retention and security

We keep routine contact enquiries and their attachments for no longer than 12 months after the enquiry is resolved, to handle relevant follow up. We delete them sooner where they are no longer needed. This period applies to the correspondence we control; it is not a fixed retention period for every provider’s technical records.

Where a message becomes part of an ongoing business relationship, we keep relevant correspondence for as long as it is needed for that relationship. Records subject to a legal retention obligation or needed for a specific legal claim may be kept longer for that purpose. Unnecessary copies and unrelated information should be removed.

The toy’s state lasts only for the current page session. Providers retain their own technical, security, and backup records according to their service policies, security needs, and legal obligations. Deleting an email from our inbox does not necessarily immediately remove every provider backup.

We limit the information requested to what is relevant to the enquiry. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

07Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, the GDPR provides rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port personal information, and to object to certain processing. Consent can be withdrawn where processing relies on it. These rights have conditions and exceptions.

Contact info@wellq.io to make a request. See how privacy requests are handled. You may complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or another competent supervisory authority. Learn more from the European Commission’s information on privacy rights.

08Children and future changes

The current website has no account registration or personal information form. If a child sends unnecessary personal information by email, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal where appropriate. Future games will need their own age appropriate privacy information before launch.

We will update this notice when our website or data practices change and communicate material changes where required. The date above identifies the latest revision.
