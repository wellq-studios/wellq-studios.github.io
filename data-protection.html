02Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you rights to:

  • Access: ask whether personal information about you is processed and receive a copy and related information.
  • Correction: ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected.
  • Erasure: ask for information to be deleted where the legal conditions apply.
  • Restriction: ask for processing to be limited in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: receive information you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine readable format where the applicable conditions are met.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation. You may object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

These rights have conditions and exceptions. For example, some records may need to be retained to comply with a legal obligation or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

03Make a privacy request

Email info@wellq.io with “Privacy request” in the subject. Describe what you would like to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to. Where applicable, you can also request portability or withdraw consent.

Requests must be answered without undue delay and normally within one month. Where the GDPR permits an extension for complexity or volume, the organisation must explain the extension within that first month. Only proportionate information should be requested to verify identity. You do not need to send a passport with an initial request.

You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or the relevant supervisory authority where you live or work.

04What to include

Tell us which right you wish to exercise and provide enough context to locate the relevant information, such as the email address you used to contact us and the approximate date of your message. Please share only what is needed to handle the request.

The website toy keeps progress only in the current page memory. Its code does not send your score or gameplay history to the studio, so it does not create a stored player record for us to retrieve.

Privacy requests are normally free of charge. If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, the GDPR allows a reasonable fee or refusal subject to its conditions and an explanation.

05Independent guidance

For more information, visit the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or read the European Commission’s guidance on privacy rights.
